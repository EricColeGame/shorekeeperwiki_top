Shorekeeper is one of the most flexible supports in Wuthering Waves, and also one of the few characters whose stat sheet can make or break a team's damage. Get her Shorekeeper ER requirements wrong and her Stellarealm uptime collapses, taking the Crit Rate and Crit DMG your main DPS was counting on right along with it. Nail the Shorekeeper ER requirements, though, and every rotation turns into a free damage buff that costs you almost nothing.

This guide breaks down what her Energy Regen actually does, the benchmarks worth building toward, and the weapons, echoes, and rotations that get you there without wasting a single substat.

## Why Energy Regen Is the Stat That Defines Shorekeeper

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier and serves as the Guardian of the Black Shores — the enigmatic "Second Instance" who appears when the Tethys System faces a crisis. In combat, every part of her kit funnels into a single resource: Resonance Energy. Her Resonance Liberation, End Loop, is expensive to cast, and her Inherent Skill Self Gravitation converts Energy Regen above the baseline into Crit Rate and Crit DMG for the whole team.

That combination is what makes her unusual. For most supports, Energy Regen is a comfort stat — nice to have, never mandatory. For Shorekeeper, it is a damage stat. Every point of ER pays off twice: once by keeping her Stellarealm active, and again by inflating the Crit buffs her teammates lean on. Miss the Shorekeeper ER requirements and you lose both at the same time.

| Ability | Type | Why It Matters for Energy Regen |
|---|---|---|
| Origin Calculus | Basic Attack | Fills gaps between cooldowns and generates energy while she is on field |
| Chaos Theory | Resonance Skill | Core of her healing loop and a fixed part of every rotation |
| End Loop | Resonance Liberation | The expensive cast your entire ER budget exists to fund |
| Astral Chord | Forte Circuit | Her Forte-gauge payoff between Liberations |
| Life Entwined | Inherent Skill | Passive support value with no energy cost attached |
| Self Gravitation | Inherent Skill | Converts Energy Regen above the baseline into team Crit buffs |
| Proof of Existence | Intro Skill | Free entry utility every time she swaps in |
| Binary Butterfly | Outro Skill | Delivers her team buff at the moment she swaps out |

A quick framing note: her Stellarealm is the healing-and-buff field that anchors her rotations, while Astral Chord is the payoff window between Liberations. Proof of Existence and Binary Butterfly slot into the swap pattern you will repeat in every fight. None of it functions if End Loop is not ready when you need it.

## Shorekeeper ER Requirements: The Benchmarks Worth Building Around

If you remember one number from this guide, remember this: community testing suggests her ER-scaling Crit buffs top out at roughly 250% Energy Regen. That is the ceiling, not the entry fee. Most players land between 200% and 250% and clear endgame content comfortably.

| Energy Regen | What It Feels Like (Community Testing) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Below 180% | End Loop regularly misses a rotation and buffs fall off | Rework the build |
| 180–200% | Workable in energy-rich teams with an ER weapon | Budget minimum |
| 200–230% | Reliable uptime, strong but not maxed Crit buffs | Recommended for most players |
| 230–250% | Near the ceiling of her ER-scaling buffs | Ideal for endgame |
| 250%+ | Buff conversion is capped; extra ER does nothing | Stop and reallocate |

Treat those ranges as player experience rather than gospel. They come from community reports gathered across different teams, weapons, and echo sets, and your own results will shift depending on how much energy the rest of your team generates for her.

### Why 250% Energy Regen Is the Number Everyone Quotes

Shorekeeper's Inherent Skill turns Energy Regen above 100% into team-wide Crit Rate and Crit DMG. Because that conversion has a cap, there is a point where additional ER stops adding damage. Community testing consistently places that point around 250%. Below it, you are leaving buff value on the table. Above it, you are spending stat budget on nothing.

### When You Can Get Away With Less

You do not need to chase the cap on every account. If your team generates energy quickly — frequent swaps, energy-friendly teammates, short fights — 200% to 220% often feels identical in practice. Early-game players with limited echo options should focus on hitting 200% reliably before chasing the final few percent.

## How to Reach Your ER Target: Weapons, Echoes, and Substats

| Source | Impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rectifier weapon substat | Very high | Her signature 5-star is ER-focused; the 4-star Variation is the budget pick |
| Echo main stats | High | 3-cost slots are the usual place to slot Energy Regen% |
| Echo substats | Medium, stacks fast | ER% rolls across several pieces add up quickly |
| Echo set bonus | Medium | Support sets such as Rejuvenating Glow reinforce her healing role |
| Team energy generation | Variable | Fast-swap teams feed her noticeably more energy |

Weapons are the single biggest swing. Shorekeeper's signature 5-star Rectifier is built around Energy Regen, and equipping it can move your total by more than 20% in one step. If you do not have it, the 4-star Rectifier Variation is the standard budget answer because its substat is Energy Regen itself.

| Echo Cost | Main Stat Priority | ER Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| 4-cost | Crit Rate, Crit DMG, or Healing Bonus | Usually reserved for buff or healing value; ER comes from elsewhere |
| 3-cost | Energy Regen% | Primary ER source — most builds run it here |
| 1-cost | ATK% or HP% | No ER, but keeps her healing and damage floor steady |

When you are rolling echoes, treat an Energy Regen substat as a keeper on any piece that already has a useful main stat. Rolling a full support set is a grind, and tossing a piece with ER on it is one of the most common ways players stall out short of their Shorekeeper ER requirements.

## Team Composition and Rotation: How Your ER Needs Change

Energy Regen is not a fixed target — it is a target relative to your team. The more energy your other characters generate and the more often you swap, the less Shorekeeper has to carry on her own stat sheet.

| Team Context | Suggested ER | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hypercarry with one long-field DPS | 230–250% | Fewer swaps mean less energy flowing to her |
| Fast-swap or dual-DPS teams | 200–230% | Frequent skills and swaps generate extra energy |
| Early game with limited echo options | 180–200% | Fewer ER sources available; prioritize uptime |
| Overworld and short fights | 180%+ | Fights often end before a second Liberation |

The standard rotation looks like this: swap in with Proof of Existence, cast Chaos Theory, spend the Forte gauge on Astral Chord, fire End Loop, then leave with Binary Butterfly into your main DPS. Do not rush her on-field time — the energy she generates while she is active is part of the budget you are building around. Cutting her field time short is a quiet way to fall below your Shorekeeper ER requirements even when the numbers on paper look fine.

One more consideration: if your rotation keeps her off field for long stretches, budget closer to 250%. She cannot generate energy she is not present for.

## Common Mistakes That Wreck Shorekeeper's Energy Regen

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| End Loop not ready each rotation | ER too low | Move a 3-cost to ER% or change weapons |
| Team Crit buffs feel weak | ER below the buff ceiling | Push toward 250% |
| ER above 250% with no visible gain | Over-investment | Reallocate a roll into Crit, ATK, or HP |
| Dying during her setup | No survivability support | Adjust echoes or bring a defensive teammate |

Beyond the table, watch for these habits:

- **Stacking ER on every single piece.** Once you pass the cap, those rolls are dead weight that could have been Crit or HP.
- **Copying a build without checking the weapon.** An ER substat on a Rectifier can be worth more than three echo rolls combined.
- **Testing ER in one fight.** Run a full rotation in a boss encounter before deciding your Energy Regen is fine.
- **Ignoring the rest of the team.** Shorekeeper's ER needs shrink when her teammates generate energy efficiently.

## FAQ

**What are the ideal Shorekeeper ER requirements?**
Community consensus points to roughly 250% Energy Regen as the point where her ER-scaling Crit buffs max out. For most players, 220% to 240% is a realistic and effective target that balances uptime with the rest of her stats.

**Can Shorekeeper work with 200% Energy Regen?**
Yes, especially in fast-swap teams that generate energy quickly. At 200% you will have reliable End Loop uptime in most content, but you will not be squeezing the full Crit buff out of her kit. It is a solid starting point, not a finishing point.

**Do I need her signature weapon to hit these targets?**
No, but it helps enormously. Her signature 5-star Rectifier is built around Energy Regen, and the 4-star Variation is the most common budget substitute because its substat is ER. Without either, you will lean harder on 3-cost echo main stats and ER substats.

**Is there such a thing as too much Energy Regen on Shorekeeper?**
Yes. Once her buff conversion caps out, extra ER does nothing for your team's damage. If you find yourself well past the cap, move those rolls into Crit, ATK, or HP so the rest of her kit keeps pace with her energy generation.
