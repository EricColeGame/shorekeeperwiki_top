Shorekeeper is one of the most plug-and-play supports in Wuthering Waves. She heals, she buffs, and she slots into nearly any team — which is exactly why so many players go searching for a **Shorekeeper F2P weapon** the moment they pull her. Her signature 5-star Rectifier lives behind a limited banner, but you genuinely do not need it.

Finding the right **Shorekeeper F2P weapon** really comes down to one stat: Energy Regen. Get that right and her rotations feel effortless. Get it wrong and her ultimate sits on cooldown while your main DPS twiddles their thumbs. This guide breaks down every free option, the stat targets that actually matter, and how to build around a weapon you already own.

## Why Shorekeeper's Weapon Slot Works Differently

Shorekeeper is a Spectro support who wields a Rectifier. She is not a damage dealer, and treating her like one is the fastest way to waste resources. Her value comes from healing and from her Outro Skill, which passes Crit Rate and Crit DMG buffs to whoever she swaps into.

That reshuffles the entire weapon priority list. Attack, Crit stats, and damage bonuses do almost nothing for her. What she wants is Energy Regen to keep her Resonance Liberation cycling, plus HP to strengthen her healing output.

| What Shorekeeper Wants | What She Ignores |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Regen (ultimate uptime) | Base ATK and ATK% |
| HP% and flat HP (healing) | Crit Rate / Crit DMG on the weapon |
| A cheap, reliable passive | Elemental DMG bonuses |
| Low investment cost | Combo-heavy damage passives |

This is why a free 4-star Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat can outperform an expensive 5-star damage stick. The stat line beats the rarity every single time.

For the overwhelming majority of players, the best Shorekeeper F2P weapon is **Variation**. ## Stat Priority for a Shorekeeper F2P Weapon Build

Because her kit converts Energy Regen into offensive value for the team, stacking ER is not just a comfort choice — it is her core scaling stat. Player experience consistently points to the same thresholds.

| Stat | Priority | Community-Reported Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Regen | Highest | Roughly 230–250% total |
| HP% | High | Stack on 3-cost and 1-cost echoes |
| Flat HP | Medium | Useful filler on substats |
| Crit Rate / Crit DMG | None | Ignore on a pure support build |
| ATK% | None | Ignore entirely |

A few things to keep in mind:

- **Energy Regen does double duty.** Her Inherent Skill converts excess ER into Crit DMG for herself, and her Outro buffs scale off her ER as well. More ER means stronger buffs, not just faster ultimates.
- **HP keeps your team alive.** Healing scales with her max HP, so HP% rolls are never wasted.
- **Numbers shift between versions.** Always confirm current values in-game or on the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/) before committing heavy resources.

## Upgrade Path: Level, Refine, and Rotate

One of the nicest things about building a support is that you can skip the most expensive steps.

1. **Level the weapon to a comfortable baseline first.** Base ATK is largely irrelevant for Shorekeeper, so there is no rush to max it. Spend that waveplate on echoes instead.
2. **Push toward your Energy Regen target.** If you are short, switch a 3-cost echo to ER before you touch the weapon.
3. **Refine Variation if you have copies.** The Concerto restoration improves with refinement, which tightens her rotation considerably.
4. **Practice the loop.** Resonance Skill into Resonance Liberation into Outro is the bread and butter. Her ultimate should be ready again by the time you cycle back.
5. **Only then max the weapon level.** Once echoes are settled, finishing the weapon is a clean, low-effort stat gain.

A Shorekeeper F2P weapon does not need to be level 90 on day one, and pretending otherwise is how players burn through tuners and credits they needed elsewhere.

The pattern behind every one of these is the same: players treat a support like a damage dealer. Once you accept that her weapon is a stat delivery system rather than a damage multiplier, the whole build becomes straightforward.

## FAQ

**What is the best Shorekeeper F2P weapon?**
Variation is the consensus pick. It provides an Energy Regen substat plus Concerto restoration, which directly supports the stat her entire kit scales from. Call of the Abyss is the closest runner-up.

**Can I use a 3-star Rectifier on Shorekeeper?**
Yes, early on. Rectifier of Voyager carries an Energy Regen substat, so it can actually beat a mismatched 4-star while you are still farming. Swap it out once a better option drops.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community reports generally place the comfortable range around 230–250% total. Below that, her ultimate uptime suffers and your team loses buff coverage during burst windows.

**Does weapon level matter for a Shorekeeper F2P weapon?**
Less than you would think. Base ATK is nearly worthless on a support, so prioritize echoes and Energy Regen first, then finish the weapon when your resources allow.

**Is Stellar Symphony required for her to work?**
No. Her signature is a luxury upgrade that offers convenience and extra stats, but a well-built free Rectifier plus a Rejuvenating Glow set will carry you through the vast majority of content in the game.

Building Shorekeeper around a free weapon is one of the cheapest high-impact projects in Wuthering Waves. Lock in an Energy Regen Rectifier, push your ER toward the community-reported target, and let her Outro buffs do the heavy lifting for your main DPS.
