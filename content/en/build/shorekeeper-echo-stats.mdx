Shorekeeper is one of the strongest supports in Wuthering Waves, and her entire kit lives or dies by her Shorekeeper echo stats. Pick the right Shorekeeper echo stats and she hands your main DPS a huge Crit Rate and Crit DMG boost while keeping the whole team alive; pick the wrong ones and she's just an expensive healer. This guide covers the best echo sets, main stats, sub-stat priorities, and Energy Regen targets so you can farm with a clear goal instead of guessing.

## What Makes Shorekeeper's Build Different From Other Supports

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier, and she occupies the healer/support slot rather than a damage role. Her kit is built around survival plus team amplification: Origin Calculus on her Basic Attack, Chaos Theory on her Resonance Skill, End Loop on her Resonance Liberation, Astral Chord on her Forte Circuit, Proof of Existence on her Intro, and Binary Butterfly on her Outro. Two Inherent Skills, Life Entwined and Self Gravitation, round out her passive support.

The catch is that her two jobs scale off two different stats. Her healing and survivability scale with HP, while the offensive buffs she passes to the incoming resonator scale with Energy Regen. That split is exactly why echo stats matter so much on her — you're not just stacking one number, you're balancing two.

| Ability | Name | Build relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Origin Calculus | Low — she is not a damage carry |
| Resonance Skill | Chaos Theory | Generates energy, sets up Forte |
| Resonance Liberation | End Loop | Healing field; scales with HP |
| Forte Circuit | Astral Chord | Core of her buff engine |
| Intro Skill | Proof of Existence | Entry damage, minor |
| Outro Skill | Binary Butterfly | Passes buffs to your next resonator |
| Inherent Skill | Life Entwined | Passive survivability |
| Inherent Skill | Self Gravitation | Passive stat support |

Because so much of her power sits in buffs rather than personal damage, you should treat her like a stat-stick support. Every echo roll that doesn't feed HP or Energy Regen is a roll that isn't helping your team kill things faster.

## The Best Echo Sets for Shorekeeper

Rejuvenating Glow is the default answer, and for good reason: it boosts healing and then converts that healing into a team-wide ATK buff, which stacks nicely with the Crit buffs Shorekeeper already provides. Moonlit Clouds is the main alternative when you're short on Energy Regen.

| Sonata Effect | Pieces | Effect summary | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rejuvenating Glow | 5 | Boosts healing, then buffs team ATK after you heal | Default choice for nearly every team |
| Moonlit Clouds | 5 | Energy Regen plus an ATK buff passed through the Outro | When you can't hit your ER target |
| Rejuvenating Glow 2 + Moonlit Clouds 2 | 2 + 2 | Healing bonus plus flat Energy Regen | Cheap transitional setup while farming |
| Celestial Light | 5 | Spectro DMG bonus | Not recommended — she isn't a DPS |

A common mistake is chasing the Spectro damage set because Shorekeeper is a Spectro unit. Don't. Her personal damage is a rounding error next to the buffs she provides, and a Rejuvenating Glow set will out-perform it in every realistic team.

## Main Stats: What to Roll on Each Echo Slot

Shorekeeper uses the standard five-echo spread of one 4-cost, two 3-costs, and two 1-costs, for a total cost of 12. Here's how player experience suggests filling each slot.

| Slot | Cost | Recommended main stat | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4-cost (main echo) | 4 | Healing Bonus or HP% | Bell-Borne Geochelone is the usual pick |
| 3-cost | 3 | Energy Regen or HP% | Go ER first if you're under target |
| 3-cost | 3 | Energy Regen or HP% | Duplicate the first 3-cost |
| 1-cost | 1 | HP% | Flat HP is acceptable early on |
| 1-cost | 1 | HP% | HP% scales her healing directly |

Your choice of main echo is the single biggest decision. Community reports consistently favor Bell-Borne Geochelone because it grants a shield and amplifies team damage at the same time, which is exactly the kind of double duty a support wants.

| Main Echo | Cost | Why players run it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bell-Borne Geochelone | 4 | Shield plus team damage amplification — the default support pick |
| Fallacy of No Return | 4 | Team ATK buff, strong in ATK-hungry compositions |
| Mourning Aix | 4 | ATK buff for the incoming resonator |
| Any 4-cost with HP% | 4 | Budget placeholder while you farm the real thing |

If you're early in progression, don't stall on the perfect 4-cost. Slot anything with HP% or Healing Bonus, clear your content, and upgrade later. The difference between a "good" main echo and a "perfect" one is far smaller than the difference between having a main echo and not having one.

## Sub-Stat Priority and Energy Regen Targets

Sub-stats are where most Shorekeeper builds quietly fall apart. Because she doesn't scale with ATK, a beautifully rolled ATK% echo is a dead item on her.

| Priority | Sub-stat | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Energy Regen | Directly feeds her buff conversion |
| 2 | HP% | Scales healing and shield strength |
| 3 | Flat HP | Small but free value |
| 4 | Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Only relevant on damage-leaning builds |
| Skip | ATK%, DEF%, Elemental DMG | No meaningful scaling for her role |

Energy Regen deserves its own conversation, because it's the stat that separates a functional Shorekeeper from a great one. Player experience suggests these rough bands:

| Energy Regen | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Below 180% | Buffs feel weak; you'll notice longer clear times |
| Around 200% | Comfortable baseline for most players |
| Around 230% | Strong; most endgame teams are happy here |
| Around 250% | Community-reported sweet spot where her conversion effectively caps |

Treat those numbers as community-reported benchmarks rather than gospel. Exact caps and breakpoints can shift with balance patches, so it's worth double-checking her in-game skill descriptions after a major update. If you can't hit the top band, don't panic — hitting 200–230% with solid HP% rolls will still feel great.

## How to Farm Shorekeeper Echo Stats Efficiently

Farming for Shorekeeper echo stats is less about grinding harder and more about grinding smarter. Her stat requirements are narrow, which actually works in your favor once you know what to look for.

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Lock any Energy Regen sub-stat on HP-scaling echoes, even off-set ones |
| 2 | Farm the 4-cost main echo first — it's the biggest single upgrade |
| 3 | Fill both 3-cost slots with ER or HP% before chasing perfect 1-costs |
| 4 | Level echoes in stages (5 → 10 → 15 → 20) and stop if the first rolls miss |
| 5 | Recycle failed echoes into EXP instead of hoarding them |

A few practical habits that save a lot of time:

- **Stop early on bad rolls.** If an echo's first two sub-stats aren't Energy Regen or HP%, cut your losses and move on.
- **Don't chase Crit on a support build.** Crit rolls only matter if you're deliberately building her for damage, which isn't her job.
- **Keep spare Energy Regen pieces.** Meta shifts happen, and an ER-heavy echo you saved today may be exactly what a future support needs.
- **Bank Echo EXP before a farming session.** Running out of EXP mid-session is the fastest way to waste a good drop.

If you want to confirm current drop pools and event details before you commit a farming session, the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) is the most reliable place to check patch notes and announcements.

## Weapons and Teammates That Complement Her Build

Echo stats don't exist in a vacuum — your weapon choice can cover gaps your echoes can't. Shorekeeper uses Rectifiers, and the ones that matter for her are the ones that provide Energy Regen or HP.

| Weapon | Rarity | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stellar Symphony | 5★ | Signature option; HP and Energy Regen in one package |
| Variation | 4★ | Accessible Energy Regen source for budget builds |
| Any Rectifier with Energy Regen | 3–4★ | Fine as a placeholder while you pull for better |

On the team side, Shorekeeper slots into almost any composition because her buffs are generic rather than element-locked. She shines brightest alongside a hyper-carry main DPS who benefits from stacked Crit stats, and she pairs well with a second support or sub-DPS who can capitalize on the ATK buff from Rejuvenating Glow. Pair her with units that already want Crit Rate and Crit DMG, and you'll feel the difference immediately.

One more note on her Resonance Chain, since it interacts with how much you need to invest in echoes. Her sequence nodes — Unspoken Conjecture, Night's Gift and Refusal, Infinity Awaits Me, Overflowing Quietude, Echoes in Silence, and To the New World — generally make her stronger at her existing job rather than changing it. That means your echo build stays the same regardless of how many copies you have; you just get more out of the same stats.

## FAQ

**What are the most important Shorekeeper echo stats?**
Energy Regen comes first, followed by HP%. Energy Regen feeds the offensive buffs she passes to your team, while HP% scales her healing and survivability. Everything else is secondary.

**Does Shorekeeper need Crit Rate or Crit DMG on her echoes?**
Not for a standard support build. She isn't a damage dealer, so Crit rolls on her echoes are largely wasted. Put those stats on your main DPS instead.

**Which main echo should I use for Shorekeeper?**
Bell-Borne Geochelone is the community favorite because it provides both a shield and a team damage boost. Fallacy of No Return and Mourning Aix are solid alternatives depending on your team composition.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community reports point to roughly 250% as the sweet spot where her buff conversion caps out, with 200–230% being a comfortable range for most players. Verify the current cap in-game after major patches, since balance changes can shift these numbers.

**Is Moonlit Clouds ever better than Rejuvenating Glow?**
Only as a stopgap. Rejuvenating Glow's team ATK buff is too valuable to give up long-term, but Moonlit Clouds can help you hit Energy Regen targets while you finish farming a proper set.
