## Why Shorekeeper's Ideal Stats Look Nothing Like a DPS Build

Shorekeeper is the rare support whose value has almost nothing to do with her own damage numbers — and that is exactly why so many players build her wrong. Getting **shorekeeper ideal stats** right means ignoring ATK, Crit Rate, and Crit DMG on her personal sheet and pouring almost everything into Energy Regen and HP instead.

In this guide, we'll walk through the **shorekeeper ideal stats** that actually matter: how much Energy Regen she needs before her buffs stop growing, what HP total keeps a team alive through tower floors, which Echo sets and main stats get you there, and which weapons solve the build without costing you a fortune.

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier, and her role is simple to describe and hard to perfect. She deploys a healing field, hands the team a Crit Rate and Crit DMG boost, and then passes an all-attribute DMG buff to whoever she swaps into. None of that scales off her own attack power. It scales off how often she can cast and how much HP she has when she does.

That's the mental shift. You are not building a character who deals damage. You are building a character who makes everyone else's damage bigger.

## Energy Regen and HP: The Two Numbers That Decide Everything

### Energy Regen: The Stat You Cannot Skip

Shorekeeper's Resonance Liberation creates the healing field that anchors her whole kit, and the buff value attached to it grows with her Energy Regen. That single sentence explains why the stat is non-negotiable — it is doing double duty as both a rotation stat and a buff-scaling stat.

Community reports consistently place the practical ceiling around 250% Energy Regen. Past that point, extra Energy Regen only buys you rotation comfort, not more team damage.

| Energy Regen | Effect on the Build | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 180–200% | Liberation returns slowly; buff windows have gaps | Playable but clunky |
| 210–230% | Near-permanent field uptime, buffs close to capped | Strong mid-game goal |
| ~240–250% | Community-reported sweet spot for buff scaling | Ideal target |
| 260%+ | Buffs stop growing; only comfort improves | Diminishing returns |

A useful rule of thumb: two Energy Regen main stats on your 3-cost Echoes plus an Energy Regen weapon substat will get most accounts into the 230–250% range without any heroic substat farming.

### HP: The Number That Keeps Your Team Standing

Shorekeeper's healing scales off her HP, which makes HP% the second pillar of the build. Unlike Energy Regen, there is no hard cap here — more HP simply means bigger heals and a wider safety margin on harder content.

| HP Range | Healing Output | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 25,000 | Thin heals; risky in Tower of Adversity | Early game only |
| 30,000–35,000 | Comfortable sustain for most content | Reachable with HP% 1-costs |
| 35,000–40,000 | Strong; shrugs off chip damage and DoT | Typical endgame target |
| 40,000+ | Generous overheal and a great safety net | Needs good substats or signature weapon |

Substat priority follows the same logic: Energy Regen% until you approach the cap, then HP%, then flat HP, then DEF% as pure filler.

## Rotation, Teams, and Resonance Chain Priorities

Stats only matter if the rotation actually uses them. Shorekeeper's buffs are attached to her field and her Outro Skill, so the order you press buttons in decides how much of that value your DPS actually receives.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Intro Skill (Proof of Existence) | Free heal and entry damage |
| 2 | Resonance Skill (Chaos Theory) plus basics | Builds Forte Circuit stacks |
| 3 | Resonance Liberation (End Loop) | Deploys the healing field and team crit buffs |
| 4 | Outro Skill (Binary Butterfly) | Passes the DMG buff to your next Resonator |
| 5 | Swap to your main DPS | Burn the buff window immediately |

The single most common rotation error is lingering on Shorekeeper after her Outro Skill is ready. Every second she stays on field after that is a second your carry is not using her buffs.

## FAQ: Shorekeeper Ideal Stats and Build Questions

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper need?**
Community testing points to roughly 250% as the practical target where her buff scaling effectively caps out. Around 230% is a perfectly reasonable stopping point while you farm better Echoes, and anything past 250% is mostly rotation comfort.

**What are the shorekeeper ideal stats for a budget account?**
A Variation Rectifier, a 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow set, Energy Regen on both 3-cost Echoes, and HP% on both 1-costs. That combination typically lands you near 230% Energy Regen and 30,000+ HP without any lucky rolls.

**Does Shorekeeper need Crit Rate or Crit DMG on her own gear?**
No. She is a support, not a damage dealer, so Crit stats on her sheet do almost nothing. Only consider Crit DMG on a 4-cost Echo if your Energy Regen is already well past the cap and your HP is comfortably high.

**Which is more important, Energy Regen or HP?**
Energy Regen, without question. HP determines how much she heals, but Energy Regen determines how often she heals *and* how strong the crit buffs she gives the team are. Fix Energy Regen first, then stack HP.
