Shorekeeper is one of the few Wuthering Waves Resonators whose value is measured almost entirely by what she gives everyone else. Her healing, her buffs, and her team-wide amplification all trace back to a handful of stats, which means a **Shorekeeper weapon** is not a damage stick — it is the throttle on your entire rotation. Choose the wrong one and your ultimate window shrinks, your buffs thin out, and every damage dealer on the team quietly loses output.

Pick the right one, and a support who never tops a damage meter becomes the reason your clears get faster. This guide breaks down what a **Shorekeeper weapon** actually needs to do, which stats are worth chasing, and how to build her without burning your Astrite on a banner you do not need.

## Shorekeeper Weapon Basics: Type, Role, and Scaling

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator from the Black Shores, described in the official lore as the guardian who emerges in response to the Rover's summons. In combat terms, she is a pure support: a healer and buffer who enables the rest of the team rather than carrying damage herself.

| Attribute | Shorekeeper |
| --- | --- |
| Element | Spectro |
| Weapon type | Rectifier |
| Combat role | Support / healer / buffer |
| Primary scaling stat | HP |
| Secondary priority | Energy Regen |
| Signature weapon | 5-star Rectifier (Stellar Symphony) |
| Debut banner | Version 1.3, September 2024 |
| Rerun window | Version 2.2, April 2025 |

Because she is a Rectifier user, her weapon pool is narrower than a sword or broadblade character's. Rectifiers split roughly into three design families: Energy Regen tools, HP-scaling tools, and ATK/crit damage tools. Only the first two fit Shorekeeper's kit. If you want to browse her official kit details and banner history, the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/) is the best place to start.

Her kit names matter when you are evaluating weapon passives. Her Resonance Skill is Chaos Theory, her Resonance Liberation is End Loop, her Forte Circuit is Astral Chord, and her Outro Skill is Binary Butterfly. A weapon passive that triggers on Resonance Skill casts or Outro swaps will fit her rotation far better than one that triggers on Basic Attacks, because she spends very little time auto-attacking.

## Which Stats Actually Matter on a Shorekeeper Weapon

Here is the single most important idea in this entire guide: Shorekeeper's weapon is a stat delivery system for her ultimate, not for her personal damage. That flips the usual priority list on its head.

| Priority | Stat | What it does for her | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Energy Regen | Refills End Loop faster, keeping her buff field online | Must-have |
| 2 | HP% | Scales her healing and her buff values | High value |
| 3 | Flat HP | Small, free contribution to HP scaling | Minor but welcome |
| 4 | Healing Bonus | Bigger heals only, no buff gain | Situational |
| 5 | ATK% / Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Almost no return for a support | Skip |

Energy Regen sits at the top because Shorekeeper's usefulness is tied to her Liberation uptime. If End Loop is not ready when your damage dealer's window opens, the whole rotation desynchronizes. Player experience consistently reports that Energy Regen is the stat people under-invest in first, and the one that produces the biggest felt improvement once fixed.

HP% comes second because it is the multiplier on her support output. A weapon with an HP% substat and a modest passive will usually outperform a flashier weapon with crit stats, simply because crit does nothing for a character who is not dealing the damage.

One caution: do not treat any single Energy Regen number as a universal breakpoint. Community reports vary wildly because the right threshold depends on your team's Concerto generation, your Echo substats, and whether you run a second support. Build to feel — if End Loop is ready on every swap cycle, you have enough.

## Best Shorekeeper Weapon Options by Budget

You do not need her signature weapon to make her work. Below is a tiered look at the realistic options, from maximum investment down to placeholder gear.

| Tier | Option | Weapon class | What it brings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best overall | Signature Rectifier (Stellar Symphony) | Limited 5-star | HP% and Energy Regen with a passive that supports the whole team |
| Strong alternative | Standard-banner Rectifier (Cosmic Ripples) | Standard 5-star | High base stats and useful Energy Regen, but an ATK-leaning passive |
| Best budget | Energy Regen Rectifier (Variation) | 4-star | Energy Regen substat plus a support-friendly passive — the community favorite |
| Healing focus | HP-substat Rectifier | 4-star | HP% substat for larger heals when Energy Regen is already covered |
| Early game | Any 3-star Rectifier | 3-star | A placeholder until you pull something better |

**The signature option.** Stellar Symphony is built for her: HP and Energy Regen in one package, plus a passive that gives something back to the team. It is the cleanest fit, but it is also the most expensive. Pull it only if you are already committed to her as a long-term roster staple.

**The standard-banner option.** Cosmic Ripples is what most players will have first. Its base stats are high and it helps with Energy Regen, but its passive leans toward personal damage that Shorekeeper cannot use. It is a fine stat stick and a genuine upgrade over any 4-star.

**The budget option.** Variation is the weapon that shows up in the majority of free-to-play Shorekeeper builds. The Energy Regen substat addresses her most important need, and the passive is oriented toward support play rather than damage. Community reports repeatedly name it the best value pick in her slot.

**The healing option.** If your Energy Regen is already solved through Echoes, an HP-substat Rectifier pushes your healing and buff values higher. This is a refinement decision, not a starting point.

## How to Build Around Your Shorekeeper Weapon

A weapon does not exist in isolation. These are the pieces that determine whether your Shorekeeper weapon actually performs.

| Build Slot | Recommended Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon substat | Energy Regen first, HP% second |
| 4-cost Echo | Energy Regen main stat if available, otherwise HP% or Healing Bonus |
| 3-cost Echo | HP% or Energy Regen |
| 1-cost Echo | HP% |
| Echo substats | Energy Regen > HP% > flat HP |
| Echo set | Healing- or support-oriented set (for example, Rejuvenating Glow) |

**Rotation matters as much as gear.** A well-geared Shorekeeper with a sloppy rotation will underperform a modestly geared one with a clean loop.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Build Concerto on another unit | Sets up a fast swap |
| 2 | Swap in and cast Chaos Theory | Starts her skill cooldown early |
| 3 | Trigger End Loop | Opens the heal and buff window |
| 4 | Use her Forte Circuit, Astral Chord | Extends the field's duration |
| 5 | Swap out with Binary Butterfly | Passes the buff to your damage dealer |

**Team fit.** Shorekeeper shines alongside burst-window damage dealers who want to unload inside a short, buffed period. Pair her with units whose biggest hits land during her Liberation window, not with characters who deal damage continuously and care little about timing.

## Common Shorekeeper Weapon Mistakes to Avoid

- **Chasing crit weapons.** A crit Rectifier looks impressive on a stat sheet and does almost nothing for a support who never deals meaningful damage.
- **Treating Energy Regen as optional.** It is the one stat that determines whether her kit functions. Everything else is a bonus.
- **Ignoring passive trigger conditions.** A passive that requires Basic Attacks will rarely fire on Shorekeeper. Read the trigger, not just the numbers.
- **Over-refining the wrong weapon.** Refinement materials are finite. Spend them on the weapon you will actually keep equipped for months.
- **Pulling the signature before testing the budget pick.** Try the 4-star Energy Regen Rectifier first. Many players find it good enough and redirect those pulls elsewhere.
- **Forgetting her banner history.** She has been featured in multiple Event Convenes since her September 2024 debut, so if you missed her, a rerun is a realistic path rather than a permanent loss.

## FAQ

**What weapon type does Shorekeeper use?**
Shorekeeper is a Rectifier user. That means she draws from a smaller weapon pool than sword or broadblade characters, and the best options for her are the ones that provide Energy Regen or HP rather than crit stats.

**Do I need her signature weapon for a good Shorekeeper weapon setup?**
No. Her signature 5-star Rectifier is the strongest option, but a 4-star Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat covers her core need at a fraction of the cost. Most players will notice the difference in refinement and Echo quality long before they notice the weapon gap.

**What is the best free-to-play Shorekeeper weapon?**
Community consensus points to a 4-star Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat, with Variation being the most frequently recommended. It solves her Liberation uptime problem, which is the single biggest performance lever in her kit.

**Does Energy Regen ever become overkill?**
Yes. Once End Loop is reliably available on every rotation cycle, extra Energy Regen produces diminishing returns, and you should shift substats toward HP% to raise her healing and buff values instead.

Build her weapon around uptime first, scaling second, and personal damage never. Do that, and Shorekeeper quietly becomes the most valuable slot on your team.
