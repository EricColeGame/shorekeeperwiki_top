## What a Shorekeeper Rerun Actually Means

In Wuthering Waves, a rerun is a limited-time Event Convene that brings back a previously released five-star Resonator. The Shorekeeper's banner is titled **Till the Sea Turns Clear**, and it has appeared in more than one version cycle. Because she is a limited unit, there is no way to obtain her through the standard Convene — no amount of lucky standard pulls will produce her.

A few practical points about how these banners work:

- **The pity counter carries over.** Pulls you make on one Event Convene count toward the next one, so saving is never wasted.
- **The 50/50 rule applies.** Your first five-star has roughly a coin-flip chance of being the featured Resonator; losing it guarantees the featured unit next time.
- **Rerun banners are shorter.** Her second appearance ran for about eleven days rather than the month-long debut window, so planning matters.
- **Four-star lineups rotate.** The featured three-stars and four-stars change between runs, which can matter if you are chasing specific supports.

Community trackers and the community-maintained wiki note that the Shorekeeper has been featured in multiple Event Convenes, though only two full windows are documented in detail. Treat any "leak" about the next one as unconfirmed until Kuro Games posts it on the official Wuthering Waves channels.

**A practical priority order:**

1. Secure one copy of the Shorekeeper.
2. Evaluate her signature Rectifier against your existing weapon inventory.
3. Only then consider early sequence nodes.
4. Skip the deep chain unless you are a dedicated spender.

**Team building tip:** pair her with a hyper-carry DPS and a sub-DPS who benefits from a longer damage window. She fits into most compositions because her kit is about enabling rather than restricting. Player experience also suggests she is comfortable in both overworld exploration and endgame challenge modes, which is unusual for a pure support.

**Resource tip:** do not convert Asterite into standard pulls while waiting. Every pull you spend elsewhere is a pull you cannot spend on the Shorekeeper rerun, and the pity counter only rewards patience.

## FAQ About the Shorekeeper Rerun

**When was the most recent Shorekeeper rerun?**
Community wiki records show her second Event Convene, "Till the Sea Turns Clear," running from April 17 to April 28, 2025 during Version 2.2, following her debut from September 29 to October 24, 2024 in Version 1.3. Dates for any future run should be confirmed through official announcements.

**Is the Shorekeeper still worth pulling on a rerun?**
For most accounts, yes. She is a sustain-and-support Resonator whose buffs and healing remain relevant across content types, and she slots into a wide range of teams rather than locking you into one composition.

**How many Astrites should I save before her banner returns?**
Plan for 12,800 Astrites to guarantee a five-star at 80 pulls, or 25,600 if you want to be safe against losing the 50/50. Saving 9,600 covers the soft-pity range many players report.

**Does her weapon banner return at the same time?**
Signature weapon banners typically run alongside the character banner they belong to, so it is reasonable to expect them together. Always verify the exact schedule rather than assuming.

**Will the Shorekeeper rerun be shorter than her debut?**
Her second run lasted roughly eleven days compared with the debut's roughly four weeks, so a short window is a fair assumption. Start farming materials early.

The bottom line: a Shorekeeper rerun is not a "maybe later" banner. She is a limited unit with no standard-pool fallback, she has appeared only a handful of times, and her windows have been getting tighter. Set your budget now, track your pity honestly, and you will be ready the moment Till the Sea Turns Clear returns.
