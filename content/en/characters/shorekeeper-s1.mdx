Shorekeeper S1 — the first Sequence Node on Wuthering Waves' Spectro support — is the upgrade most players start researching the moment they finish building her. Her entire kit exists to make the rest of the team hit harder, so any node that scales those buffs pays off across three characters at once instead of one. If you already own her and you're weighing a second copy, this guide breaks down what Shorekeeper S1 actually adds, who should chase it, and when stopping at S0 is the smarter call.

## Who the Shorekeeper Is, and Why Her Nodes Matter

The Shorekeeper is a playable Spectro Resonator in Wuthering Waves. She is known as "the Second Instance," the Guardian of the Black Shores, and she is described as a mythical being formed from Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals. According to her lore, she guides the Tethys System, appears only when crisis strikes, and serves the true leader of the Black Shores. Even inside her own organization, she remains a deeply enigmatic figure.

What matters for your account, though, is what she does in combat. She is not a damage dealer. She is a healer and a buffer — the kind of unit whose value shows up in the numbers your *other* characters produce.

| What she provides | Source in her kit | Why players care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team healing | Her healing-oriented Forte abilities | Keeps fragile carries alive in endgame content |
| Team-wide crit support | The field effect tied to her Resonance Liberation, End Loop | Multiplies damage for every attacker on the team |
| Outgoing damage buff | Her Outro Skill, Binary Butterfly | Passes bonuses to whoever swaps in next |
| Rotation stability | Her Forte Circuit, Astral Chord | Makes quick-swap teams feel smooth |

Because almost all of her value is transferred to teammates, a Sequence Node that improves her buffs is effectively a buff for your entire roster. That is the core reason Shorekeeper S1 gets discussed so often.

## What Shorekeeper S1 Actually Changes

In Wuthering Waves, the Resonance Chain is the upgrade track that unlocks when you obtain duplicate copies of a five-star Resonator. Each duplicate unlocks the next node, so reaching S1 — player shorthand for Sequence Node 1 — requires exactly one extra copy of the character.

Her first node is named **Unspoken Conjecture**. Community reports consistently describe it as the highest-value stop on her entire chain for support-focused accounts, because it deepens the crit-oriented support her field already provides rather than adding damage she never needed.

Here is the full chain, using the official node names:

| Node | Name | How most players treat it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Unspoken Conjecture | The standard stopping point; best value per copy |
| S2 | Night's Gift and Refusal | Pursued mainly by dedicated Shorekeeper players |
| S3 | Infinity Awaits Me | Diminishing returns for a pure healer/support role |
| S4 | Overflowing Quietude | Long-term investment territory |
| S5 | Echoes in Silence | Rarely reached outside heavy spending |
| S6 | To the New World | Maximum investment; a different build philosophy |

### S0 vs. S1 at a glance

| Aspect | S0 (base) | S1 (one duplicate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Elite healer and crit buffer | Same role, higher ceiling |
| Team damage contribution | Strong | Stronger, felt in every rotation |
| Build changes required | None | None — same Echoes, same weapon |
| Pull cost | One limited five-star | One additional limited five-star |
| Priority for F2P accounts | High | Situational |

The practical takeaway: S1 does not change *how* you play her. It raises the value of the buffs you were already passing to your team. For players chasing clear times in endgame modes, that compounding effect is the whole point.

## Who Should Pull for Shorekeeper S1

Not every account should spend pulls on a duplicate. Limited five-star copies are expensive, and a future character may serve you better. Use this table as a rough decision filter.

| Your situation | Recommendation | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand-new account with one decent carry | Skip | A second limited character adds more than a node |
| Mid-game with two functioning teams | Consider | S1 raises the ceiling of both teams at once |
| Endgame player chasing faster clears | Yes | Crit support scales with every invested carry |
| Shorekeeper main / collector | Personal choice | S2–S6 exist for players who love the character |
| F2P saving for an announced favorite | Skip | Reruns return; you can revisit S1 later |

If you fall into the "consider" row, a good rule is to secure the duplicate only after you have enough pulls left to guarantee your next must-have character. Sequence Nodes are a luxury upgrade, not a requirement — she is already a top-tier support at S0.

## Banner History: When Copies Are Actually Available

The Shorekeeper is a limited five-star, which means her copies are only obtainable while her featured Convene is live. You cannot farm her Sequence Nodes the way you can for standard-banner characters, so planning around her rerun windows matters.

According to community wiki records, she has appeared in the following featured banners:

| Banner | Version | Dates | Featured four-stars |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Till the Sea Turns Clear | 1.3 | September 29 – October 24, 2024 | Taoqi, Yangyang, Chixia |
| Till the Sea Turns Clear (rerun) | 2.2 | April 17 – April 28, 2025 | Baizhi, Sanhua, Aalto |

Two patterns are worth noting. First, reruns are typically shorter than debut banners — the 2025 window ran only about two weeks — so you need your pulls ready *before* the banner opens. Second, the four-star lineups changed between runs, which means your duplicate-pulling session can also fill out different support options for your account.

If you are planning for Shorekeeper S1, keep an eye on the [official Wuthering Waves site](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/) and in-game announcements for the next Convene schedule. Community trackers are useful, but the official channel is what confirms dates.

## Building Around Shorekeeper S1: Teams, Echoes, and Rotation

The good news is that S1 does not demand a rebuild. The same investment you would make at S0 carries straight over.

### Team archetypes that benefit most

| Archetype | Example carries | Why she fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spectro hypercarry | Jinhsi | Crit support amplifies a single big-damage window |
| Havoc hypercarry | Camellya | Keeps a melee carry alive while buffing burst damage |
| Glacio hypercarry | Carlotta | Smooths rotations for a carry that wants clean setups |
| Quick-swap teams | Xiangli Yao, Changli | Her healing and outro buff reward fast swapping |

### Investment priorities

| Priority | What to build | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Energy Regen | Keeps her Resonance Liberation available on schedule |
| 2 | Healing-focused Echo set | Player experience points to sets like Rejuvenating Glow as the community default |
| 3 | HP% and Healing Bonus main stats | Improves survivability and healing throughput |
| 4 | Any high-Energy-Regen Rectifier | She does not need a damage weapon to do her job |

### A simple rotation habit

1. Build Forte stacks with her basic attacks and skill.
2. Trigger End Loop so her field effect is active before your carry commits.
3. Use her Outro Skill to hand buffs to your main damage dealer.
4. Swap in, burst, and repeat — her healing covers the gaps.

Community reports suggest that most players see the biggest jump from S1 simply by tightening this rotation, not by changing gear. If your buffs are not lining up with your carry's damage window, a Sequence Node will not fix that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Shorekeeper S1 worth it for most players?**
For endgame-focused accounts with invested carries, yes — Shorekeeper S1 is generally treated as the best value node on her chain because it strengthens team-wide buffs rather than personal damage. For newer or F2P accounts, a new character usually beats a duplicate.

**How many copies do I need for Shorekeeper S1?**
You need one extra copy beyond your first. The first copy unlocks the character; the second unlocks Sequence Node 1, "Unspoken Conjecture." Each further duplicate unlocks the next node.

**Does S1 change her build or weapon?**
No. She still wants Energy Regen, healing-oriented Echoes, and a Rectifier with a supportive passive. S1 raises her buff ceiling without altering stat priorities.

**Can I unlock her Sequence Nodes without pulling on her banner?**
No. As a limited five-star, her copies only come from her featured Convene while it is active, so you must plan around rerun windows rather than farming her nodes passively.

## Final Thoughts

The Shorekeeper was designed as a force multiplier, and her first Sequence Node leans directly into that identity. If your carries are already built and your rotations are clean, one extra copy is one of the most efficient upgrades you can buy in Wuthering Waves. If your roster is still growing, keep her at S0, enjoy one of the best supports in the game, and revisit Shorekeeper S1 when her next banner returns.
