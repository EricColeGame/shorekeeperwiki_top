## Who Is the Shorekeeper?

The Shorekeeper is a playable Spectro Resonator in Wuthering Waves, and her in-game identity explains a lot about her appeal. She is known as "the Second Instance," the Guardian of the Black Shores, and is described as a mythical entity composed of Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals.

According to the game's lore, she has the power to guide the Tethys System and control the stars themselves, appearing only during times of crisis to aid the true leader of the Black Shores. Even among the organization's own guests and members, she remains an incredibly enigmatic figure — which is a big part of why her banner generates so much excitement.

## What to Expect When the Shorekeeper Banner Returns

Rerun convenes in Wuthering Waves generally follow the same structure as a debut banner, with a few practical differences worth understanding.

### Featured Four-Star Pool

The four-star lineup changes between runs, as the history above shows. On her debut, players pulled alongside Taoqi, Yangyang, and Chixia. Her rerun swapped in Baizhi, Sanhua, and Aalto — a much more support- and sustain-oriented pool.

Community reports suggest this rotation is deliberate: reruns often pair a limited five-star with four-stars that complement her team role. If you already have Taoqi or Baizhi at high sequence, the value of the banner shifts accordingly.

### Pity, Guarantees, and Carryover

| Mechanic | How It Typically Works |
| --- | --- |
| Pull currency | Astrite converted into convene pulls |
| Pity counter | Carries over between limited character banners |
| Guarantee | A lost 50/50 typically guarantees the next limited five-star |
| Banner separation | Character convene pity does not apply to the weapon convene |

Because pity carries across limited character banners, pulls you spend on another five-star before the Shorekeeper banner returns are not wasted. Many players deliberately build pity on a banner they can live without, then finish the job when the Shorekeeper returns.

### The Companion Weapon Convene

Character banners are usually accompanied by a weapon convene featuring that Resonator's signature weapon. Weapon pity is tracked separately, so if you want both the character and her weapon, plan two budgets — not one. Player experience consistently suggests prioritizing the character first, since the Shorekeeper's value comes primarily from her role on the team rather than raw damage output.

## FAQ

**When does the Shorekeeper banner return?**

There is no fixed schedule. Her documented runs were in Version 1.3 (September–October 2024) and Version 2.2 (April 2025). Kuro Games confirms reruns only through official announcements, so watch patch livestreams for the Shorekeeper banner to appear in the next version's convene lineup.

**Is the Shorekeeper banner worth pulling for a new account?**

For most new players, yes. She is a flexible Spectro support who fits into many team compositions, which means she keeps contributing even as your roster grows. The main exception is if you are already saving for a specific upcoming character.

**Does pity carry over between Shorekeeper banner runs?**

Yes. Pity on limited character convenes carries over between banners, including reruns. That means pulls spent on other limited banners still count toward your next five-star, and a lost 50/50 typically guarantees the next limited character you pull.

**What element and role is the Shorekeeper?**

She is a Spectro Resonator known in lore as the Second Instance and Guardian of the Black Shores. Community consensus places her in a support role, which is why she is often recommended for accounts that need a durable, flexible backbone rather than another pure damage dealer.

**Do I need her signature weapon too?**

Not necessarily. The weapon convene uses separate pity, so chasing both doubles your budget. Player experience suggests securing the character first and treating the weapon as an optional follow-up if your Astrite allows it.
