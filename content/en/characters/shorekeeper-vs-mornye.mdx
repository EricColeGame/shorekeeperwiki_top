## Shorekeeper at a Glance: The Guardian of the Black Shores

The Shorekeeper is a playable **Spectro** Resonator in *Wuthering Waves*. In the game's lore she is known as "the Second Instance," the Guardian of the Black Shores, and a mythical entity formed from Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals. Her official introduction describes her as a divine being who governs the essence of aloofness, emerging only in response to your summons after eons of guardianship.

She is also described as having the power to guide the Tethys System and control the stars themselves, appearing during times of crisis to aid the true leader of the Black Shores. Even among the organization's own guests and members, she remains an incredibly enigmatic figure — which is a big part of why players connect with her so strongly.

### Story Presence and Banner History

The Shorekeeper is deeply tied to the main story rather than being a standalone event character. She features in Chapter I Act VIII ("To the Shore's End"), the Chapter II prologue ("Through the Sea Thou Break"), and the Chapter III prologue ("When the Unknown Thrums"), along with event appearances such as *Cube, Cubic n Cubie* and *Memory Travelogue*.

Her first Event Convene, "Till the Sea Turns Clear," ran from September 29 to October 24, 2024 during Version 1.3, alongside Taoqi, Yangyang, and Chixia. A rerun of the same banner ran April 17–28, 2025 in Version 2.2 with Baizhi, Sanhua, and Aalto. If you missed both windows, that history tells you reruns do happen — but they can be well over a year apart.

The element difference is the most concrete separator. You are choosing between two different damage or support ecosystems — and whichever one your existing roster already supports is usually the smarter investment.

### Team-Building Implications

- **Check your existing Fusion core first.** If you already run Encore, Mortefi, or another Fusion unit with investment, Mornye slots into an ecosystem you have already paid for.
- **Check your Spectro core second.** Shorekeeper pairs naturally into Spectro-leaning compositions and teams that want a dedicated support presence, per community reports.
- **Do not double-build supports.** If you already have a strong support character leveled, adding a second one rarely doubles your output.
- **Weapon and Echo overlap matters.** Shared weapon types and Echo sets reduce the total farming cost of adding a new character.

### Four Questions to Ask Before You Pull

1. **Does this character fill a hole, or fill a duplicate slot?** A duplicate role is the most common wasted pull in any gacha game.
2. **Can I afford the full build, not just the pull?** Ascension Materials, Forte upgrades, and Echo farming are the real cost.
3. **Do I have a weapon that fits?** A character without a decent weapon underperforms immediately.
4. **Am I pulling for power or for the character?** Both are valid reasons — just be honest about which one is driving you.
