## Who the Shorekeeper Is, and Why Her Story Matters

The Shorekeeper is a playable Spectro Resonator and the Guardian of the Black Shores. In the game's own framing she is called "the Second Instance," a mythical being composed of Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals. Her power is not purely combative: she guides the Tethys System and is described as having influence over the stars themselves.

She only manifests during times of crisis, and only to aid the true leader of the Black Shores. Even among the organization's own members and guests, she remains an enigmatic figure — which is precisely what makes her story arc so effective.

The emotional core of the Shorekeeper quest material is transformation. Having spent eons in pure guardianship, she begins to develop desire, attachment, and a determination to connect with the world — and with you specifically. That arc is why so many players describe her storyline as the most affecting in the Black Shores region.

## FAQ

**Do I need to complete any events to understand the Shorekeeper quest?**
No, but it helps. The main quest chapters cover her essential arc, while events like Memory Travelogue add emotional depth that players who skip them tend to miss.

**Can I recruit the Shorekeeper without finishing her story chapters?**
Recruitment has historically been tied to limited banners rather than quest completion, so it is possible to field her before seeing all her scenes — though the story reads better in order.

**How long does the Shorekeeper quest material take to get through?**
It depends on how much side content you do. The relevant acts are a handful of main quest segments, but the surrounding Black Shores exploration and events can add many hours.

**Is the Shorekeeper quest worth doing if I do not main her?**
Yes. Her storyline carries major Black Shores lore, including details about the Tethys System and the region's guardianship, which pay off in later chapters regardless of your team composition.
