The Shorekeeper is one of the most flexible supports in Wuthering Waves, and a well-built Shorekeeper build guide can turn her from a niche healer into the backbone of nearly every team you run. She packs healing, Energy Regen, and one of the strongest team-wide crit buffs in the game into a single Spectro kit. Whether you just pulled her on a rerun or you are deciding whether to invest, this Shorekeeper build guide covers the Echoes, weapons, stat targets, and rotations that actually move the needle.

## Who Is the Shorekeeper? Role, Element, and Kit at a Glance

The Shorekeeper is a limited 5-star **Spectro** Resonator who wields a **Rectifier**. In the story she is known as "the Second Instance," the Guardian of the Black Shores — a mythical entity formed from Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals who only appears when the Black Shores face a crisis. In gameplay, that translates into a healer and buffer who wants almost nothing for herself and gives almost everything to her team.

Her kit is built around two ideas: keep everyone alive, and make your main damage dealer hit harder.

| Ability | Name | What It Does in Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Origin Calculus | Standard attack string; mostly used to build energy and Forte stacks |
| Resonance Skill | Chaos Theory | Deals Spectro DMG and starts the Forte loop that fuels her healing |
| Resonance Liberation | End Loop | Creates her signature healing field that follows the active resonator |
| Forte Circuit | Astral Chord | Converts accumulated stacks into healing and team support |
| Intro Skill | Proof of Existence | Entry attack that repositions her and applies Spectro damage |
| Outro Skill | Binary Butterfly | Carries her buffs forward to the next resonator when she swaps out |
| Inherent Skill | Life Entwined | Passive that improves her support output |
| Inherent Skill | Self Gravitation | Passive tied to her Energy Regen scaling |

Two scaling axes matter here, and both are widely reported by the community: her **healing scales with HP**, while her **team buff scales with Energy Regen**. That single detail shapes every gear decision below.

## Best Echoes for a Shorekeeper Build

Echo choice is where most players either nail this build or waste weeks of farming. Because she is a support first and a damage dealer a distant second, you want sets that amplify healing and team damage — not her personal numbers.

| Echo Set | Pieces | Why It Works | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rejuvenating Glow | 5 | Healing Bonus for her, plus a team ATK buff after she heals | The default, safest pick |
| Moonlit Clouds | 5 | Boosts the incoming resonator's ATK after an Outro Skill | Quick-swap and double-support comps |
| Celestial Light | 5 | Spectro DMG bonus | Personal-damage builds only — not recommended |
| Mixed 2-piece sets | 2+2+2 | Energy Regen and HP filler | Early game, before you can farm a full set |

For most accounts, **Rejuvenating Glow** is the answer. It smooths out her healing and hands your carry a free ATK boost, which is exactly the job description.

### Echo Main Stats and Substats

| Slot | Recommended Main Stat | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-Cost | Fallacy of No Return | Community-favorite support Echo; buffs team ATK |
| 4-Cost (alternative) | Bell-Borne Geochelone | Extra shielding if your team is squishy |
| 3-Cost | Energy Regen | Directly feeds her buff scaling |
| 3-Cost | Energy Regen or HP% | Second 3-cost slot |
| 1-Cost | HP% | Healing output scales with HP |
| 1-Cost | HP% | Same as above |

Substat priority is refreshingly simple for a support:

- **Energy Regen** until you hit your target
- **HP%** and flat HP after that
- **Crit Rate / Crit DMG** only if you want her personal damage, which is optional
- **Healing Bonus** from the set itself, not from substats

## Best Weapons and Stat Targets

Weapons are the easiest place to overspend. If you are free-to-play, you do not need her signature weapon to make this Shorekeeper build guide's advice work.

| Weapon | Rarity | Why It Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stellar Symphony | 5★ | Her signature; HP and Energy Regen with a team-wide buff |
| Variation | 4★ | Widely recommended budget pick thanks to its Energy Regen substat |
| Any high Energy Regen Rectifier | 3★–4★ | Serviceable placeholder while you farm |

If you already own a strong 5-star Rectifier from another character, test it before pulling. Energy Regen is the stat that matters most, and plenty of cheaper options deliver it.

### Stat Targets to Aim For

| Stat | Community-Reported Target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Regen | Roughly 250% | Commonly cited as the point where her crit buff caps out |
| HP | As high as possible after ER | Directly improves healing |
| Healing Bonus | From the 5-piece set | Multiplies everything she heals |
| Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Optional | Only relevant for personal damage builds |

Treat the Energy Regen number as a goal rather than gospel — check the in-game ability description for exact values, since balance patches can shift them. In practice, most players report that once Energy Regen is covered, dumping everything else into HP feels best.

## Team Compositions and Rotation

The Shorekeeper slots into almost any team because she does not compete for field time. She drops her field, buffs the team, and leaves. That makes her the default third slot in a huge range of comps.

### A Simple Rotation Anyone Can Learn

1. Enter with her **Intro Skill** to start applying Spectro damage.
2. Cast her **Resonance Skill** to open the Forte loop.
3. Build stacks with basic attacks until her Forte Circuit is ready.
4. Cast her **Resonance Liberation** to place the healing field.
5. Swap out with her **Outro Skill** so the buff transfers to your carry.

One detail that trips up new players: her healing field follows whoever is on the field, not a fixed spot. You do not need to stand still — keep attacking and let the field trail you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Shorekeeper worth building if I already have another healer?**
Usually yes. Her value is not just healing — it is the crit buff she hands your main damage dealer. Teams that run her typically clear content faster than teams running a pure healer, which is why this Shorekeeper build guide keeps coming back to Energy Regen over raw healing stats.

**What Energy Regen should a Shorekeeper build guide target?**
Community testing commonly points to around 250% as the sweet spot where her buff maxes out. Below that, you lose team damage; far above it, you are wasting substats you could spend on HP.

**Which Echo set is best, Rejuvenating Glow or Moonlit Clouds?**
Rejuvenating Glow is the safer, more universal pick because the healing bonus helps her keep the team alive while the ATK buff helps your carry. Moonlit Clouds is a fine alternative if you run a quick-swap team and want the outgoing resonator to hit harder.

**Can she work as a main damage dealer?**
Not really. Her multipliers are low and her kit is designed around support. You can build her for Spectro damage, but you will get far more value putting those resources into a dedicated DPS and letting her do what she does best.

**When does she return to banners?**
She is a limited 5-star, so she only appears during her featured Convene banners — the "Till the Sea Turns Clear" runs are the ones to watch. Save your pulls and check the official schedule before spending.

Build her right and the Shorekeeper quietly becomes the most valuable support on your account — a healer who makes everyone else stronger.
