## Why Shorekeeper Changes How You Build a Team

Shorekeeper is the rare support who makes everyone else on your roster hit harder. If you pulled her during her debut or a later rerun, the next question is always the same: what does the ideal Shorekeeper team actually look like? The short answer is that she fits almost anywhere — but a handful of compositions squeeze far more value out of her kit than the rest. This guide breaks down the strongest Shorekeeper team setups, how to rotate them cleanly, and which budget options still work when a key five-star is missing from your account.

She debuted on September 29, 2024 during version 1.3 and has returned on later banners, so plenty of players now have her sitting at low investment with no clear plan. That is a waste. Shorekeeper is one of the few units who scales a team's ceiling rather than just patching its weaknesses.

## What Shorekeeper Brings to a Team

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator known as the Guardian of the Black Shores — a healer and buffer whose kit is built around keeping the field stable while amplifying the damage of whoever comes next. Her value is not raw personal damage; it is the crit-focused buff package she hands over when she leaves the field.

Her ability names, as documented on community wikis, are worth knowing because they map directly onto how you play her:

| Ability | Type | Role in a Shorekeeper team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin Calculus | Basic Attack | Fills downtime and helps build her Forte gauge |
| Chaos Theory | Resonance Skill | Core setup tool and healing trigger |
| End Loop | Resonance Liberation | Creates a persistent field effect that sustains the team |
| Astral Chord | Forte Circuit | Enhanced healing output once her gauge is charged |
| Life Entwined | Inherent Skill | Passive sustain bonuses |
| Self Gravitation | Inherent Skill | Passive stat support for her own scaling |
| Proof of Existence | Intro Skill | Entry damage and heal when she swaps in |
| Binary Butterfly | Outro Skill | Team-wide crit buff and healing when she swaps out |

The takeaway: her Outro Skill is the reason she exists on your team. Crit Rate and Crit DMG buffs apply to every damage dealer simultaneously, which means a single Shorekeeper investment pays off across your entire account rather than one character.

### Why Crit Buffs Matter So Much

Most damage dealers in Wuthering Waves are chasing crit rolls on every Echo. A support who hands out crit stats for free frees your DPS to stack ATK and damage bonus instead. That is the mechanical reason a Shorekeeper team tends to out-damage the same team with a pure healer in her slot.

## The Best Shorekeeper Team Compositions

The formula is consistent: one main damage dealer, one sub-DPS or coordinated-attack partner, and Shorekeeper. What changes is which element you are buffing and how much field time your DPS needs.

### Jinhsi + Shorekeeper

This is the pairing most players think of first. Jinhsi wants teammates who can trigger coordinated attacks or apply different Spectro-adjacent effects to build her Incandescence, and Shorekeeper's buff window lines up neatly with Jinhsi's charged burst. If you own Zhezhi, she is the natural third slot; Yinlin works if your Zhezhi is committed elsewhere.

### Camellya + Shorekeeper

Camellya's damage is heavily crit-dependent, which makes Shorekeeper's Outro Skill disproportionately valuable here. Sanhua remains the cheapest strong partner thanks to her fast rotation and basic-attack amplification. Community reports consistently rank this trio among the highest-damage teams available to most accounts.

### Jiyan + Shorekeeper

Jiyan spends a long time in his liberation state, so a support who buffs for the whole window rather than a few seconds is ideal. Mortefi provides coordinated attacks; Aalto is a serviceable budget stand-in.

## Rotation: How to Actually Play the Team

A Shorekeeper team lives or dies on rotation order. Her buff only applies when she swaps out, so you never want to end a fight with her on field.

| Step | Action | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Shorekeeper Intro Skill into Resonance Skill | Apply setup and start healing |
| 2 | Basic attacks to charge Forte Circuit | Prepare enhanced healing |
| 3 | Resonance Liberation | Establish the field effect |
| 4 | Swap out with Outro Skill | Deliver the crit buff to your DPS |
| 5 | Main DPS burst window | Spend the buff on your biggest damage |
| 6 | Sub-DPS outro chain | Hand off to the next buffer |
| 7 | Return to Shorekeeper | Refresh the buff before it expires |

Keep her field time short. Every second Shorekeeper spends attacking is a second your damage dealer is not. In practice, a clean rotation has her on field for a few seconds at most.

## Echo, Weapon, and Stat Priorities

Shorekeeper's scaling rewards Energy Regen and HP, so build her as a support first and resist the urge to give her damage stats.

| Slot | Recommended | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-Cost Echo | Fallacy of No Return | Community-favored for its team ATK buff |
| 3-Cost Echoes | Energy Regen main stats | Prioritize hitting her ER breakpoint first |
| 1-Cost Echoes | HP% main stats | Improves healing and survivability |
| Sub-stats | Energy Regen > HP% > Flat HP | Healing Bonus is a fine bonus roll |
| Set options | Rejuvenating Glow or Moonlit Clouds | Healing-focused vs. team-buff focused |

| Stat | Priority | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Regen | Highest | Guarantees her liberation every rotation |
| HP% | High | Directly scales her healing |
| Crit stats | Low | She is not a damage source |
| ATK% | Low | Minimal impact on her support value |

Player experience suggests aiming for enough Energy Regen to comfortably cast her liberation each cycle; after that, stack HP and stop. Over-investing in crit stats on Shorekeeper is one of the most common build mistakes.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Leaving Shorekeeper on field too long.** Her damage is negligible; her buff is everything.
- **Skipping her Outro Skill.** Swapping out with the Outro is the entire point of the character.
- **Building crit on Shorekeeper.** Those stats belong on your DPS.
- **Ignoring Energy Regen.** Without it, her liberation window breaks and the rotation collapses.
- **Running double healers.** Unless you are learning a fight, the second healer slot is better spent on damage.
- **Forgetting the field effect refreshes.** Re-cast her liberation before the buff expires in longer fights.

## FAQ

**What is the best Shorekeeper team for a new player?**
Start with Rover (Havoc), Danjin, and Shorekeeper. All three are accessible, the rotation is forgiving, and the team clears most story and farming content without heavy investment.

**Does Shorekeeper work with any main DPS?**
Yes. Because her buff is crit-based rather than element-locked, she slots into Spectro, Havoc, Aero, Fusion, Electro, and Glacio teams. She is arguably the most flexible support in the game.

**How much Energy Regen does a Shorekeeper team need?**
Enough for her to cast her Resonance Liberation every rotation. Player testing generally treats a comfortable Energy Regen threshold as the first build goal, then moves to HP% for healing.

**Is Shorekeeper worth pulling if I already have Verina?**
For most accounts, yes. Verina is excellent, but Shorekeeper's crit buffs raise team damage ceilings in a way that pure healing does not, and having both lets you field two strong supports across different teams.

**Can a Shorekeeper team clear endgame content?**
Yes. With a properly invested main DPS and clean rotations, a Shorekeeper team handles tower-style endgame modes comfortably — the buff uptime matters more than raw character levels.

## Final Thoughts

Shorekeeper is not a character you build around; she is a character you build *through*. Pick your strongest damage dealer, add a sub-DPS who matches their element, and let Shorekeeper handle sustain and crit amplification. Get her Energy Regen right, keep her field time short, and always leave with her Outro Skill active. Do that, and your Shorekeeper team will outperform setups that cost far more to assemble.
