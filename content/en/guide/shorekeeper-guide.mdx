## Shorekeeper Overview: Why This Spectro Support Matters

The Shorekeeper is a 5-star Spectro Resonator and a Rectifier user who debuted in version 1.3 of Wuthering Waves. In the story she is known as "the Second Instance" and the Guardian of the Black Shores — an entity formed from Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals who guides the Tethys System and appears only when the Black Shores face a crisis.

Her combat value is easier to explain than her lore. She is a healer whose buffs scale with her own Energy Regen, which means she rewards investment in a stat most damage dealers ignore completely. Since her release, community consensus has placed her among the strongest support units in the game, largely because she fits into almost any team without demanding a specific element partner.

## Shorekeeper Kit and Abilities Explained

Understanding the kit matters more than memorizing multipliers, because her value comes from *when* you press each button rather than how hard each button hits.

| Ability | Type | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin Calculus | Basic Attack | Standard attack string; also a way to build Forte when your skill is on cooldown |
| Chaos Theory | Resonance Skill | Deals Spectro damage and kicks off her Forte generation |
| End Loop | Resonance Liberation | Deploys the Stellarealm, her signature healing field |
| Astral Chord | Forte Circuit | Spends stored Forte to trigger her healing effects |
| Proof of Existence | Intro Skill | Her entry attack when she swaps onto the field |
| Binary Butterfly | Outro Skill | The team-wide buff she leaves behind when she swaps out |
| Life Entwined / Self Gravitation | Inherent Skills | Passives that reinforce her healing and Energy Regen |

### How the Stellarealm Actually Works

The Stellarealm is the heart of her kit. Cast End Loop and a field appears around the active Resonator — and critically, it **follows them** as they move, dodge, or swap characters. Because it persists after the Shorekeeper leaves the field, you can drop it early in a rotation and let your main damage dealer enjoy it for their entire damage window.

Community reports suggest both the healing output and the field's effectiveness improve as you invest in her level, skills, and stats. That is why a well-built Shorekeeper feels dramatically better than a half-leveled one, even though she never appears in your damage charts.

### Energy Regen Is Her Real Damage Stat

The Outro Skill, Binary Butterfly, buffs whichever Resonator swaps in after her, and community testing indicates the size of that Crit Rate and Crit DMG bonus scales with her total Energy Regen up to a cap. In plain terms: every Energy Regen roll on her Echoes is potentially extra damage for your carry. That single fact shapes every build decision below.

## Best Shorekeeper Build: Echoes, Stats, and Weapons

Her build looks nothing like a DPS build, and that trips up a lot of new players. The goal is simple — stack Energy Regen, keep her alive, and let the buffs do the work.

| Echo Slot | Community-Typical Main Stat | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4-Cost | Healing Bonus (Energy Regen as an alternative) | Improves her sustain and helps her burst come back faster |
| 3-Cost | Energy Regen | Directly feeds her Outro buff scaling |
| 3-Cost | Energy Regen | Doubling up is common in support builds |
| 1-Cost | HP% | Her healing and survivability are reported to scale from HP |
| 1-Cost | HP% | Same reasoning as above |

| Substat Priority | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Energy Regen | Powers her Liberation uptime and Outro buff |
| 2. HP% | Improves healing and keeps her off the floor |
| 3. Flat HP | Cheap filler that still helps |
| 4. Crit DMG / ATK% | Low priority — she is not a damage dealer |

| Echo Set Option | Pieces | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rejuvenating Glow | 5-piece | The standard healer setup; boosts team damage while healing |
| Rejuvenating Glow + Moonlit Clouds | 2 + 2 | Mixed builds that want a little extra Outro utility |
| Moonlit Clouds | 5-piece | Outro-buff-focused setups in coordinated teams |

For the 4-cost slot, community guides frequently point to defensive support Echoes such as Bell-Borne Geochelone, or a Black Shores support Echo that grants a team-wide attack buff. Both are valid; pick based on whether your team needs survivability or raw damage.

On weapons, her signature 5-star Rectifier is the straightforward upgrade because it is tuned for Energy Regen. Free-to-play players can lean on 4-star Rectifiers with an Energy Regen substat — community reports commonly cite Variation as the accessible pick — and treat any Energy Regen Rectifier as a fine placeholder until something better drops.

## Best Teams and Rotation for the Shorekeeper

The Shorekeeper is a universal slot. She does not care whether your carry is Spectro, Havoc, or Glacio, which is exactly why she shows up in so many endgame teams.

| Team Archetype | Main DPS | Sub-DPS / Enabler | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spectro hypercarry | Jinhsi | Zhezhi | Long damage windows love a persistent Stellarealm |
| Aero hypercarry | Jiyan | Mortefi | Her healing covers Jiyan's aggressive uptime |
| Havoc hypercarry | Camellya | Sanhua | Great fit for teams that ignore incoming damage |
| Electro hypercarry | Xiangli Yao | Yinlin | Buffs land cleanly on his burst window |
| Glacio hypercarry | Carlotta | Zhezhi | Works in both quick-swap and hypercarry styles |

These pairings reflect community-standard team building; swap the sub-DPS for whoever you actually own. The constant is the Shorekeeper in the third slot.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Swap in with Proof of Existence | Free damage and Forte on entry |
| 2 | Cast Chaos Theory | Starts her Forte cycle |
| 3 | Add basic attacks if Forte is short | Fills the gap without wasting field time |
| 4 | Cast End Loop | Drops the Stellarealm onto your carry |
| 5 | Use Astral Chord if the team is low | Emergency healing without losing the field |
| 6 | Swap out with Binary Butterfly | Lands the Crit buff on your main DPS |
| 7 | Stay on your carry | The Stellarealm follows them automatically |

A few practical habits make a real difference. Do not overstay on the Shorekeeper — her buffs are front-loaded, so a long field visit is wasted time. Also try to time her Outro so the incoming character is your damage dealer, not a sub-DPS who is about to leave.

## FAQ

**Is the Shorekeeper still worth pulling?**
Yes. Community consensus has stayed consistent: she is a universal healer and buffer who slots into nearly every team. If you lack a strong support, she is one of the safest long-term investments in the game.

**What is the best Echo set for the Shorekeeper?**
Most players run a 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow set with Energy Regen and HP% main stats. A 2-piece Rejuvenating Glow plus 2-piece Moonlit Clouds mix is a reasonable alternative if you want more Outro utility.

**Does she need her signature weapon?**
No. Her signature Rectifier is a meaningful upgrade, but any Rectifier with an Energy Regen substat keeps her functional. Weapon pulls are a luxury, not a requirement, for this Shorekeeper guide.

**Can the Shorekeeper work as a main DPS?**
Realistically, no. Her damage output is low and her kit is built around healing and buffing. Building her as a damage dealer wastes the Energy Regen scaling that makes her valuable in the first place.
