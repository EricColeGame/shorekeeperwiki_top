Shorekeeper is one of the most flexible support Resonators in Wuthering Waves, and a properly tuned Shorekeeper best build can lift an entire team's damage ceiling. Unlike a main damage dealer, her value comes from healing, energy generation, and team-wide crit buffs, so every stat you chase should serve those jobs and nothing else. Get the Shorekeeper best build right and she becomes a permanent slot in almost every endgame team; get it wrong and she feels like a wasted party member.

This guide covers the stat targets, weapon options, echo sets, substats, and rotations that make her shine — and explains why a few popular "obvious" choices are actually traps.

## Why Shorekeeper's Kit Shapes Her Build

Shorekeeper is a Spectro Resonator who wields a Rectifier. In the lore she is the Guardian of the Black Shores, known as "the Second Instance" — a being composed of Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals who guides the Tethys System and appears during times of crisis. Mechanically, though, she is a healer-support hybrid whose buffs scale off her own stats rather than her attack power.

That single detail is the foundation of every good Shorekeeper best build. If you build her like a damage dealer, you waste every substat on a character who will never be your primary source of damage.

Her kit is built around a simple loop: heal the team, generate energy, drop a persistent buff field, and hand a buff to whoever comes in next. Here is what each part does and how much it matters for your build.

She debuted in the "Till the Sea Turns Clear" Convene from September 29 to October 24, 2024 during Version 1.3, and returned April 17–28, 2025 in Version 2.2. ## Shorekeeper Best Build: Stat Targets and Breakpoints

Here is where most players go wrong. Shorekeeper's team buffs are tied to her Energy Regen, not her ATK. Community testing has consistently pointed to roughly 250% Energy Regen as the soft cap where her crit buffs reach their maximum value, which means Energy Regen is not a "nice to have" — it is the stat you build around.

| Stat | Target | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Regen | ~250% (community soft cap) | Maxes the crit buffs tied to her Liberation |
| HP% | As high as possible after ER | Scales her healing and survivability |
| Flat HP | Filler | Small but free healing gains |
| Healing Bonus | Optional | Comfort pick for harder content |
| Crit Rate / Crit DMG | Ignore | She is not a damage dealer |
| ATK% | Avoid | Does not scale her support value |

A practical way to think about it: Energy Regen first, HP% second, everything else is a bonus. If you can only remember one number from this Shorekeeper best build guide, make it the Energy Regen target.

### Energy Regen Without Sacrificing HP

The tension in her build is that Energy Regen and HP% compete for the same slots. The good news is that her weapon, echo main stats, and substats together can carry you to the threshold without gutting her healing.

- **Weapon substat** — a Rectifier with Energy Regen does a lot of the heavy lifting.
- **Two 3-cost echoes** — running Energy Regen on both is the standard community recommendation.
- **Substat hunting** — prioritize Energy Regen rolls on every piece until you hit the cap, then switch to HP%.

### The Standard Rotation

1. Swap in with her Intro Skill, **Proof of Existence**.
2. Cast her Resonance Skill, **Chaos Theory**, to start generating energy.
3. Use a couple of basic attacks to build Forte Circuit charges.
4. Cast her Resonance Liberation, **End Loop**, to place her healing and crit buff field.
5. Immediately swap out with her Outro Skill, **Binary Butterfly**, to pass a buff forward.
6. Start your DPS window while standing inside the field.

The biggest mistake players make is leaving her on field too long. She is a swap-in, swap-out support — her job is to set the table and leave.

### Talent Leveling Priority

Community consensus is that her talent levels matter far less than her stats do. If materials are tight, level her Liberation and Forte Circuit and move on.

## FAQ

**What is the Shorekeeper best build for free-to-play players?**
Start with a 4-star Rectifier that has an Energy Regen substat, run a 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow set with Energy Regen on both 3-cost slots, and push your substats toward roughly 250% Energy Regen. That setup costs nothing but stamina and performs close to premium options.

**How much Energy Regen does Shorekeeper actually need?**
Community testing points to about 250% as the point where her crit buffs max out. Below that, you are leaving team damage on the table; far above it, you are wasting substats that could go into HP%.

**Which echo set is better, Rejuvenating Glow or Moonlit Clouds?**
Rejuvenating Glow is the safer default because it heals and buffs at the same time. Moonlit Clouds is stronger in coordinated hypercarry teams where every buff is funneled into one damage dealer.

**Is Shorekeeper worth building if I already have another healer?**
Yes. Her crit buffs and energy support make her a damage amplifier, not just a safety net. Most players find she replaces a pure healer outright rather than competing with one.

## Final Thoughts

The Shorekeeper best build comes down to one idea: she is a stat-scaling support, not a damage dealer. Hit your Energy Regen breakpoint, stack HP%, pick an echo set that matches your team's needs, and keep her on field for as little time as possible. Do that, and she will quietly multiply every point of damage your main DPS produces — which is exactly what a Guardian of the Black Shores should do.
