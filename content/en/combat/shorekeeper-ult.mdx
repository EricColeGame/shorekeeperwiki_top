## What the Shorekeeper Ult Actually Does

Shorekeeper arrived in Wuthering Waves during the version 1.3 "Till the Sea Turns Clear" convene, which ran from September 29 to October 24, 2024, and she returned in a version 2.2 rerun from April 17 to April 28, 2025. From the day she launched, players have described her less as a healer and more as a buff engine — and the Shorekeeper ult is the reason why.

Her Resonance Liberation is called End Loop. Rather than existing as a single burst of damage, it establishes a support state around the team: it restores health to nearby Resonators and layers crit-oriented buffs onto whoever is active. Because those buffs live on a field instead of a one-time cast, the timing of the Shorekeeper ult matters as much as the stats sitting behind it.

It helps to see the whole kit in context, since every other button she presses exists to make End Loop available more often and land at the right moment.

| Ability | Name | Role in the rotation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Origin Calculus | Filler string used to build Forte between cooldowns |
| Resonance Skill | Chaos Theory | On-demand healing and the main Forte builder |
| Resonance Liberation | End Loop | The Shorekeeper ult itself — team healing plus crit buffs |
| Forte Circuit | Astral Chord | Spends built-up Forte to extend her healing and buff uptime |
| Intro Skill | Proof of Existence | Free value the moment she swaps onto the field |
| Outro Skill | Binary Butterfly | Hands her buffs forward to the next Resonator |
| Inherent Skill | Life Entwined | Passive that smooths out her healing output |
| Inherent Skill | Self Gravitation | Passive that supports her energy and field presence |

In the story, Shorekeeper is the Guardian of the Black Shores, known as "the Second Instance" — a being composed of Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals who guides the Tethys System and appears when the Black Shores needs her most. That guardian framing is a useful way to think about her gameplay: the Shorekeeper ult does not fight the battle, it keeps the battle winnable.

## Why the Shorekeeper Ult Is a Force Multiplier

Most healers in Wuthering Waves solve one problem: keeping your team alive. Shorekeeper solves that problem and then quietly solves a second one by raising the ceiling on your damage.

Crit stats are multiplicative, which means a buff to Crit Rate or Crit DMG scales everything your damage dealer does — every hit, every Resonance Skill, every ultimate. That is why community reports consistently place her near the top of support tier lists even though her personal damage is unremarkable.

| What the ult provides | Why it matters in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Team-wide healing | Removes the need for a second dedicated healer in most lineups |
| Crit Rate and Crit DMG support | Multiplies the output of every crit-scaling DPS on your roster |
| A buff field tied to the active Resonator | Buffs follow whoever is on field instead of being wasted on a swap |
| Coverage across a full damage window | Lets your main DPS unload an entire rotation inside the buff |
| One slot doing two jobs | Frees a team slot for a second damage dealer or a sub-DPS |

The practical result is a team economy advantage. Instead of running DPS plus healer plus support, many players run two damage dealers and let the Shorekeeper ult cover both survivability and amplification. That is a large part of why she has stayed relevant across multiple version updates.

## How to Charge and Rotate the Shorekeeper Ult

### Energy Regen Is the Real Cooldown

End Loop is expensive, and Shorekeeper does not generate energy particularly quickly on her own. That means Energy Regen is effectively the stat that decides how often the Shorekeeper ult is available — not her healing numbers, and not her HP.

Community reports generally rank Energy Regen first for comfort, with HP percentage close behind it, and healing bonuses after that. If your ult is still charging when your DPS window opens, the buff is late and the rotation falls apart.

| Stat priority | Why it sits there |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Regen | Determines whether End Loop is ready when your DPS is ready |
| HP% | Her healing scales off her own health pool |
| Healing Bonus | Improves comfort, but rarely fixes a broken rotation |
| Flat HP | Useful filler on Echo substats, never the goal |

### A Repeatable Rotation

Once Energy Regen feels stable, the sequence itself becomes muscle memory. The goal is simple: have the field active before your damage dealer starts spending cooldowns.

| Step | Action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Swap in with Proof of Existence | Free value with no energy cost |
| 2 | Cast Chaos Theory | Heals the team and starts building Forte |
| 3 | Basic attacks and Astral Chord | Top up Forte and energy while the skill recharges |
| 4 | Cast End Loop | Field up, healing and crit buffs live |
| 5 | Swap out with Binary Butterfly | Pass buffs forward to your DPS |
| 6 | Run the damage window, then reset | Repeat once the ult is charged again |

One tip that separates clean play from messy play: do not fire the Shorekeeper ult the instant it lights up. Hold it until your main damage dealer is about to commit to a burst window. Buffs that tick away while nobody is attacking are buffs you paid energy for and never used.

## Building Around the Shorekeeper Ult: Teammates and Stats

Shorekeeper is close to a universal support, but she pairs best with damage dealers who lean on crit and who spend a long, uninterrupted window on field. Characters with short, bursty rotations get less out of the field than characters who can stay out and keep hitting.

| Team archetype | Example damage dealers | Why the pairing works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spectro hypercarry | Jinhsi | Long on-field windows convert the crit buff into sustained damage |
| Havoc basic-attack DPS | Camellya | Rewards an extended field presence inside the buff |
| Glacio crit DPS | Carlotta | Crit-scaling kit benefits directly from the amplification |
| Electro burst DPS | Xiangli Yao | Burst windows line up neatly with the ult's uptime |
| Aero sustained DPS | Jiyan | Keeps attacking while the field is live, so nothing is wasted |

On the weapon side, Shorekeeper uses a Rectifier, and her signature option is widely recommended for players who want her healing and buff uptime to feel effortless. A healing-focused Echo set is the standard starting point for most community builds, with substats prioritized the way the table above suggests.

## Resonance Chain: Is It Worth Chasing?

Shorekeeper's Resonance Chain is a vertical investment, and for most players it is optional. The base kit already does the job the Shorekeeper ult is famous for. The sequences deepen her support identity rather than changing what she does.

| Node | Name |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Unspoken Conjecture |
| 2 | Night's Gift and Refusal |
| 3 | Infinity Awaits Me |
| 4 | Overflowing Quietude |
| 5 | Echoes in Silence |
| 6 | To the New World |

If you are deciding where to spend resources, a comfortable Energy Regen build on a base-copy Shorekeeper will outperform a high-sequence Shorekeeper whose ult never lines up with her team's damage window. Sequence levels are a luxury; rotation timing is a requirement.

Common mistakes worth avoiding:

- Casting End Loop with no DPS on field to benefit from it
- Neglecting Energy Regen because the healing numbers looked fine in the stat screen
- Forgetting to swap out with Binary Butterfly, which wastes the buff handoff
- Treating her as a pure healer and building zero offensive support value around her
- Skipping her Intro Skill, which is free value on every swap

## FAQ

**What is the Shorekeeper ult called?**
It is End Loop, her Resonance Liberation. It heals nearby Resonators and applies crit-focused buffs, making it a support ultimate rather than a damage nuke.

**Does the Shorekeeper ult deal damage or only heal?**
It contributes Spectro damage, but its real value is the field it creates — healing plus crit amplification for the team. Players bring her for the buff, not the hit.

**How much Energy Regen does the Shorekeeper ult need?**
Exact breakpoints depend on your team and Echo substats, but community reports consistently put Energy Regen at the top of her priority list. Test your rotation in the Tower of Adversity and add Energy Regen until the ult is ready before your DPS window opens.

**Is the Shorekeeper ult still worth building around?**
Yes. As long as crit-scaling damage dealers define the meta, a support who heals and multiplies their damage in one slot remains one of the safest investments on any account.
