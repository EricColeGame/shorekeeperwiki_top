## Why the Shorekeeper Kit Matters More Than Her Damage

Shorekeeper is the kind of support that quietly rewrites a team's damage ceiling, and understanding her Shorekeeper kit is the fastest way to get there. She is a Spectro Resonator from the Black Shores who heals, buffs, and stabilizes rotations all at once — which is why she keeps showing up in endgame compositions long after her debut. If you pulled her and aren't sure what to press, this guide walks through every part of the Shorekeeper kit: abilities, Resonance Chain, build priorities, and team comps, in plain language.

She is officially known as "the Second Instance" and the Guardian of the Black Shores, a being said to be composed of Sonoro Spheres and high-purity Remnant Energy crystals. Lore aside, what matters on the battlefield is simple: she keeps your team alive, then hands your main damage dealer a much bigger swing window.

| Quick Profile | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Element | Spectro |
| Combat Role | Support / Healer |
| Known As | "The Second Instance," Guardian of the Black Shores |
| Primary Scaling | HP and Energy Regen |
| Signature Mechanic | A field effect that heals and amplifies the team |
| Debut Version | 1.3, on the "Till the Sea Turns Clear" Convene |

The single most common mistake new owners make is treating her like a sub-DPS. She isn't. Her personal damage is modest by design, and every point of investment is better spent on HP, Energy Regen, and healing output — the things that keep her field active and her buffs flowing.

## Ability Breakdown: Every Button in the Shorekeeper Kit

Her Forte is built around a single idea: set up a healing field, charge it, then convert it into team-wide offense. Each ability feeds the next.

| Ability | Name | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Origin Calculus | Standard attack string; mainly used to build Forte and fill downtime |
| Resonance Skill | Chaos Theory | Core setup button — deals Spectro DMG and starts her field effects |
| Resonance Liberation | End Loop | The big team heal and buff window; the heart of the kit |
| Forte Circuit | Astral Chord | Empowered state/attack unlocked once Forte is charged |
| Inherent Skill | Life Entwined | Passive that supports her healing and sustain |
| Inherent Skill | Self Gravitation | Passive that converts stats into stronger team buffs |
| Intro Skill | Proof of Existence | Entry attack that opens her rotation cleanly |
| Outro Skill | Binary Butterfly | Passes her buffs forward to the incoming Resonator |

### Basic Attack: Origin Calculus

Origin Calculus is not where her value lives, but you will still use it. Between cooldowns, a few basic attacks help build Forte charge so you can reach her empowered state sooner. Don't over-invest in attack stats to make this hit harder — that's a trap.

### Resonance Skill: Chaos Theory

Chaos Theory is the button that starts everything. It deals Spectro damage and establishes the field effects that her later abilities build on. In practice, you want this up early in the rotation so the rest of your team fights inside it.

### Resonance Liberation: End Loop

End Loop is the payoff. Community testing consistently reports that this is where the bulk of her healing and team amplification lands, and it's the reason she slots into so many lineups. Treat it as a team-wide "reset button" — pop it when your DPS is about to commit to their biggest damage window.

### Forte Circuit: Astral Chord

Astral Chord is her empowered state. Once charged, it lets her contribute a meaningful chunk of Spectro damage while keeping the field alive. It's a nice bonus, not the main event.

### Inherent Skills: Life Entwined and Self Gravitation

These two passives are where the Shorekeeper kit gets quietly efficient. Life Entwined reinforces her sustain, while Self Gravitation rewards you for stacking the stats she actually wants — turning your build choices directly into team buffs.

### Intro and Outro: Proof of Existence and Binary Butterfly

Proof of Existence gives her a clean entry, but Binary Butterfly is the one to remember. Her Outro is what makes her a "buff and leave" support: you cycle her in, fire her kit, then swap out and let the next Resonator inherit the benefits.

## Building Around the Shorekeeper Kit: Stats, Echoes, and Weapons

Because her healing and buffs scale off HP and Energy Regen, her build is refreshingly simple compared to a DPS character. You are not chasing crit rolls for personal damage.

| Priority | Stat | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | HP% | Directly increases healing output and buff strength |
| 2 | Energy Regen | More ults = more healing and more buff windows |
| 3 | Healing Bonus | Multiplies everything her kit already does |
| 4 | Flat HP | Useful filler on otherwise dead rolls |
| 5 | Spectro DMG | Only if you genuinely want Astral Chord damage |

A few practical build notes from community reports:

- **Energy Regen is not a dump stat.** If you can't fire End Loop reliably every rotation, her whole kit stalls. Aim for a comfortable threshold before chasing anything else.
- **Echo sets:** healing-oriented sets that boost team sustain pair naturally with her, while support sets that buff the incoming Resonator after an Outro are the other popular choice. Both work — pick based on whether your team needs more survivability or more damage.
- **Weapons:** her signature 5-star option is the clear best-in-slot, but budget Energy Regen rectifiers perform admirably. Don't feel forced into the weapon banner.
- **Substats:** HP% and Energy Regen above almost everything else. Flat ATK and crit rolls are wasted on her.

If you are deciding where to spend a limited weapon pity, a support weapon is usually a worse return than a new DPS character. Shorekeeper functions fine on a 4-star.

## Team Comps and Rotation Tips

Shorekeeper is close to universal. She fits any team that wants healing plus a damage window, which in practice means almost every team in the game.

| Team Archetype | Example Core | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spectro hyper-carry | Spectro main DPS + sub-DPS + Shorekeeper | Amplifies burst windows for big single-target hits |
| Basic-attack DPS | Attack-focused DPS + buffer + Shorekeeper | Keeps the DPS on-field longer with constant healing |
| Fusion / Glacio carries | Elemental DPS + support + Shorekeeper | Universal slot — she doesn't demand a specific element |
| Dual-support | DPS + second healer/buffer + Shorekeeper | Extreme safety for hard endgame modes |

**A simple rotation to practice:**

1. Start on Shorekeeper and use Chaos Theory to establish the field.
2. Build Forte with a short basic attack string if you need Astral Chord.
3. Fire End Loop when your DPS is ready to commit.
4. Use Astral Chord if charged, then swap out.
5. Let Binary Butterfly carry her buffs onto your main damage dealer.

One nuance worth internalizing: Shorekeeper's value is in *timing*, not uptime. Firing her Liberation while your DPS is standing around doing nothing wastes the entire kit. Line it up with their burst window.

## FAQ

**Is Shorekeeper a healer or a buffer?**
Both, and that's the point. The Shorekeeper kit delivers strong healing alongside meaningful team-wide amplification, which is why she's often described as a "heal-and-buff" support rather than a pure medic.

**What stats does her kit scale with?**
Primarily HP and Energy Regen. HP drives her healing and buff strength, while Energy Regen determines how often you can fire her Liberation. Crit and ATK rolls are largely wasted.

**Do I need her signature weapon?**
No. Community reports consistently show she performs well on budget Energy Regen rectifiers. The signature is an upgrade, not a requirement.

**Is she still worth pulling today?**
For most accounts, yes. Universal supports age well because they slot into whatever DPS you happen to own. Unless you already have a stacked roster of healers, she remains one of the safest long-term investments in the game.

## Final Thoughts

The Shorekeeper kit rewards players who understand sequencing. She isn't flashy, and she won't top your damage charts — but she will make every other character on your team hit harder and survive longer. Build her for HP and Energy Regen, fire End Loop when your carry is ready to commit, and let Binary Butterfly do the rest. Master that loop and you'll wonder how you ever ran a team without her.
